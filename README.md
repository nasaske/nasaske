## What I engineer

- **Agentic systems** — multi-agent orchestration, durable memory, contextual
  retrieval, evaluations, guardrails, and recovery paths.
- **Operational automation** — event-driven workflows, scheduled jobs,
  webhooks, RPA, and human-in-the-loop operations.
- **API ecosystems** — services and SDKs connecting CRMs, Google Workspace,
  email, messaging, and data platforms.
- **Production software** — backends and dashboards backed by real data,
  observability, testing, CI, and disciplined delivery.

## Selected systems

- **Atlas** — persistent engineering intelligence connecting active development
  with durable project memory, architectural decisions, operational evidence,
  and reusable skills.
- **Automation platform** — production workflows across CRM, Gmail, Sheets,
  Drive, messaging, and Cloud Run, with scheduling, auditing, idempotency, and
  failure recovery.
- **Service libraries** — reusable Google Apps Script SDKs for Pipedrive,
  Bitrix24, ChatApp, and SendGrid with consistent contracts, retries, redaction,
  generated references, and CI.
- **AI-native workspaces** — multi-profile assistants, isolated knowledge
  boundaries, persistent sessions, CQRS backends, and role-aware access.
